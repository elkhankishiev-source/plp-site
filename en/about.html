Step by step

With you at every stage

01

We work out the task

Goal, budget, timing, whether you will rent it out. We send 3–5 options with the numbers, not the whole catalogue.

02

A 20-minute meeting

Online or in person in Phuket. We show properties, work out the yield and answer questions. No obligation.

03

We view properties

We pick you up and drive you. Not on the island? We film the site and show finished homes by the same developer.

04

We check the papers

Construction permit, land, title and the developer\u2019s record. Whatever we find, we tell you — even when it argues against the deal.

05

We reserve the unit

The reservation holds your specific unit and price while the contract is prepared.

06

Contract and payment

We go through the contract clause by clause, help with the transfer and track payments against the developer schedule.

07

Handover and keys

We inspect before handover, push for defects to be fixed, and handle the transfer and utility meters.

08

And after that

Furniture, visas, renting out and management. Income and costs live in your account, not in a chat thread.
